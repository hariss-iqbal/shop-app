IN vs OUT: OUT = our device at their shop (they owe us). IN = their device in our shop (we owe them, but only once it sells). IN devices are sellable from the normal POS, flagged consignment, and excluded from our owned-stock value. Selling an IN device books the sale (margin = our price − IN price) and adds what we owe to the partner's tab. Each partner has one net balance (they-owe − we-owe) you can settle in one go.
